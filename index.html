Add to Codex
  1. Click Add to Codex and allow your browser to open the app.
  2. Confirm the pet installation in Codex.
  3. Choose your pet in Settings → Pets, then use /pet or Show pet.

Requires the Codex desktop app with Pets enabled and an internet connection. No terminal needed.

Codex didn't open?

Update and launch Codex, then try again and allow your browser to open the app. Check that Pets is available in Settings; older versions may list it under Appearance.

You can also use an install script or read the official Pets guide.
