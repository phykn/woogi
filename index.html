Add to Codex
  1. Click Add to Codex and allow your browser to open the app.
  2. Confirm the pet installation in Codex.
  3. Choose your pet in Settings → Pets, then use /pet or Show pet.

Requires the Codex desktop app with Pets enabled on your computer. No terminal commands or ZIP downloads needed.

Codex didn't open?

Update Codex, open it once, and try again. Allow the browser's prompt to open the app. If your account or app does not have Pets, these links will not install a pet.

Older versions may list Pets under Settings → Appearance. You can also try the alternative script instructions or read the official Pets guide.
